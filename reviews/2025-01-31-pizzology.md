ordered takeaway from Pizzology the other day - it was yum! they've got a good selection of vegan fakey meat toppings and nice veeg cheese

the loaded fries with cheese and jalapenos are also A+ but I felt like I was being pretty gluttonous having a whole pizza to myself so just got the regular "steak" chips

pretty cheap, too! £26.61 delivered, a bit cheaper than Papa Johns which is our usual go-to for pizza delivery

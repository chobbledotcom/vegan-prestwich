I had lunch at "Two Birds One Stone" today in Whitefield - it was really tasty!

They didn't have a vegan breakfast on the menu, but I asked if they could swap the eggs for scrambled tofu and remove the halloumi cheese, and they did - and I added vegan black pudding too.

It was very busy in there and quite noisy, so be warned, but the staff were friendly and service was fast.

(They really should just add a vegan breakfast to their menu though - as should everywhere, obvs)

We ate at Wendy's Thai Corner next to Chips at No.8 last night - really tasty food, and there's a whole page of vegan dishes in the menu. We had the penang tofu, Thai red curry, chow mein, spicy salad - all very tasty, would recommend. And Wendy is a good host who made us feel very welcome!
